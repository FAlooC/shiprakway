Who We Are

Experience that moves from insight to execution.

SHIPRAKWAY Consulting is a specialist consulting firm focused on the rigid packaging industry. Our collective experience spans greenfield projects, manufacturing operations, PET preform and bottle technology, new product development, mould development, process optimisation and technical leadership.

We combine deep industry knowledge with hands-on execution to convert complex technical challenges into practical solutions and measurable business outcomes.

What We Do

Our Expertise

New Product Development

PET & ISBM Technology

PET preform and bottle technology, ISBM process optimisation, quality improvement and troubleshooting.
